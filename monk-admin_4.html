Common Questions
How long is the free trial?
The free trial is of 7 days. It starts as soon as you activate free trial.
How much will I be charged after the trial ends?
After the trial ends you’ll be charged $0 for the first month, post that every month you’ll be charged based on the total number of orders that the store processes in a 30 day period
Why do I approve a charge of $149?
Every store approves a maximum amount of $149. This is done to ensure that the app keeps working smoothly as your store grows. Don’t worry we won’t charged you $149, we’ll charge you based on the total store orders the store processes in a month.
How do I cancel my subscription?
After you have activated free trial, you can simply uninstall the app to cancel the subscription.
Who is eligible for the free plan?
The free plan is only available to stores with Basic shopify plan and doing between 0-50 orders for a month.
What if I’m on a Shopify plan other than Basic and have 0–50 orders?
If you are not on basic plan or do more than 50 orders that month then you’ll be charged as per the pricing band
What happens if my store goes above 50 orders?
Your pricing band will automatically adjust based on your order volume. For 51–1,000 orders/month, the charge is $49/month. For 1,000+ orders/month, the charge is $149/month.
